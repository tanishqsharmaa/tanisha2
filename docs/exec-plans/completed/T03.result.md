1. **Step-by-Step Double Ikat Visual Process Diagram (`src/components/textile/DoubleIkatProcess.tsx`):**
   - Heading: `"The Art of Double Ikat"`.
   - Rendered the 6 required steps in an illustrated editorial sequence with custom SVG loom/thread/resist-dyeing diagrams:
     1. **Design Planning** — The pattern is planned on graph/grid before the weaving process begins.
     2. **Thread Preparation** — Silk yarns are arranged and divided into warp and weft bundles according to the design.
     3. **Tying** — Selected sections of both warp and weft threads are tightly tied with cotton thread to resist dye.
     4. **Dyeing** — Threads are dyed in carefully planned stages; different sections are tied and dyed to create multiple colours.
     5. **Setting the Loom** — The prepared warp and weft threads are meticulously arranged on the traditional slant loom.
     6. **Weaving** — Both sets of dyed threads must align precisely at every intersection during weaving to form the final double-ikat pattern.
   - Provided interactive toggle between "Complete Sequence" grid view and "Step Focus" interactive walkthrough with previous/next controls.
